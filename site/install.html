Put Harmony Today on your home screen

It works like a real app — its own icon, opens fullscreen, and it updates itself. No app store, nothing to download from anywhere else. Takes about twenty seconds.

🤖 Android phone or tablet

  1. Open harmony.justsoyouknow.site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  4. Confirm — the schoolhouse icon appears with your other apps.
If Chrome shows an Install banner or the button above is visible, one tap does all of it.

🍎 iPhone or iPad

  1. Open harmony.justsoyouknow.site in Safari (it must be Safari — Chrome can't add apps on iOS).
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with the arrow pointing up, at the bottom or top of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Name it (Harmony Today is pre-filled) and tap Add.
On iOS 16.4 or newer, the installed app can even receive update notifications if we turn them on later.

🧒 A kid's tablet

Same steps on their device — sign in to Safari or Chrome once, add to home screen, done. It opens fullscreen with no address bar for them to wander, and you can restrict the browser afterward: the app keeps working because it's really the site, cached on the device. It also keeps showing lunch and the calendar even when school Wi-Fi is being school Wi-Fi.

💻 Laptop?

No install needed — it's just a website. Bookmark harmony.justsoyouknow.site and you have everything. Chrome and Edge also offer Install page as app… in the address bar if you like a separate window.

Harmony Today is a parent-run family dashboard. Updates arrive automatically whenever you open it — there's never anything to update by hand.
