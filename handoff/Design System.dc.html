Earthy / outdoor-ed direction, v1.0. Oat paper, near-white cards, one green for identity and action, three accents that encode meaning. Separation is border plus fill, never shadow. Every value here matches tokens.json.

Two rules to hold onto
1. Today is the only thing at hero scale. The date, the weather, and the lunch are the largest type on the page — nothing below Today exceeds a 24px heading.
2. Every data-driven region names its source in mono. It's how a family knows the page is alive, and where to look when a widget goes stale.
