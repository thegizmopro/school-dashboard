4. Public comment your voice

Anyone may speak briefly on any district matter — even items not on the agenda. This is the easiest way to be heard: show up, sign in if there's a card, and keep it to the usual 2–3 minutes. The board generally can't discuss or act on non-agenda items tonight, but they must listen, and items raised often reach future agendas.
Want to speak? Come to Assembly Hall by 8:40, or ask in the Meet chat how remote comment is handled.

5A. Approve Unaudited Actuals for 2025-26 money

California school years close June 30; the "unaudited actuals" are the closed books for 2025-26 — official even though an outside CPA audit comes later in the fall. Approving them authorizes no new spending; it accepts the accounting of what already happened, and the packet then goes to the county office (Education Code §42100, normally by Sep 15). This is the year-end scorecard against the adopted budget we summarized: the plan projected a ~$291K unrestricted surplus, a ~$1.21M total ending balance (~$204K unassigned), SpEd costs of ~$1.25M with a ~$958K general-fund subsidy, and funding built on 206.85 ADA. It also assumed a $650–750K architect-lawsuit settlement landing this year.
What to watch in the report: did the unrestricted fund finish above or below plan · did the lawsuit money actually arrive · actual ADA vs. the 206.85 projected (attendance is what the state pays on) · whether staff still project the district can reach 2027-28 without more cuts. The two pages that tell the story: the unrestricted general-fund summary and the components of ending fund balance. Document is public at the district office during business hours.
